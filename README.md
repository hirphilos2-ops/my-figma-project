# FITPULSE
A fitness tracking app

🔥 FitPulse – Your Fitness, Your Rhythm  
FitPulse is the ultimate fitness companion designed to keep you motivated, consistent, and in control of your health journey. With real-time activity tracking, personalized workout insights, and progress monitoring, FitPulse helps you stay on top of your goals—whether you’re running, lifting, or simply staying active.

KEY FEATURES

1. 🥗 Nutrition
Your personalized hub for fueling your body the right way. Track daily meals, calories, and macros with ease. FitPulse helps you balance nutrition with fitness goals, offering insights into how your diet supports your workouts and overall progress

2. 📈 Progress
See your journey come to life. The progress section visualizes your achievements with charts, streaks, and milestones. Whether it’s weight changes, workout consistency, or calorie burn, you’ll always know how far you’ve come and where you’re headed.

3. 👤 Profile
Your fitness identity, all in one place. Customize your profile with personal stats, goals, and preferences. FitPulse adapts to your unique fitness journey, ensuring your experience feels tailored and motivating every time you log in.

4. 💪 Workouts
Your training command center. Browse guided routines, log custom workouts, and track performance in real time. Whether you’re lifting, running, or stretching, the workouts section keeps everything organized and easy to follow.
